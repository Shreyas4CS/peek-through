A game built for conceptual thinking

Peek Through is designed by CognoSpace to help children in grades 3 to 7 think about why an answer is right, not just guess it. To find the hidden picture, players have to understand one simple rule and apply it again and again as the puzzles get harder.

+
=

A square stays open only when it is open on every window.

Spatial reasoning

Players match the same row and column across several windows, building a mental map of where each square sits.

Logical thinking

"Open on window 1 and window 2" is the AND rule, the same logic used in coding, robotics and circuits.

Elimination

Instead of guessing, players rule out blocked squares one window at a time until only the answer is left.

Visualisation

Children imagine the windows stacked together before they see it. The slide-together reveal then checks their thinking.

Step-by-step challenge

The same rule grows from 1 window to 4 and from finding 1 picture to 2, so the concept deepens without feeling hard.

Learning from mistakes

With the teacher's password, a hint greys out blocked pictures for 10 seconds (3 per theme). Wrong answers show the correct one, so every try teaches something.
